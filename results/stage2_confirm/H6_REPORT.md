## 7. The part-2 trust gate and the tripwire

> **Frozen bar, quoted verbatim** (PREREGISTRATION_AMENDMENT_2_ADDENDUM_A.md precondition 5 part 2, carrying PREREGISTRATION_AMENDMENT_2.md B2.2's bar):
>

If part 2 fails the bar, H6 scoring halts pending rubric revision.”

**Result: PASS.** Raw agreement **0.8833** against the ≥ 0.85 bar; Cohen's κ **0.7667** against the ≥ 0.60 bar, over 120 rows from 60 confirmatory subjects. The verdict was applied mechanically by `experiments/h6_part2_score.py`, which was committed before any co-audit label existed.

**Deviation carried into this gate (D3 pattern, owner-directed).** The auditor line is a blind Opus 5 co-audit, substituted for the owner's own labels. It is reported as its own line and never pooled with a human line — no human line exists for it.

**The 120-row sizing ruling.** The frozen text sets a floor of ≥ 60 rows, not a ceiling. The tranche was first built at 60 and the owner ruled it up to 120 **before any co-audit label existed**, so the enlargement adds power without adding bias. The reason on the record: development's FOLLOW-UP overturn rate was 25%, sitting between the 20% and 35% lines, and a 30-row FOLLOW-UP tranche measures that rate in steps of 3.3 points — enough sampling noise to produce a false halt or a false all-clear. Full note: `results/stage2_openended/h6_part2_build_note.md`.

> **Frozen bar, quoted verbatim** (results/stage2_openended/H6_B3_PARAM_SPEC_DRAFT.md section 4.3(c), APPROVED 2026-07-28):
>
> “Part-2 FOLLOW-UP overturn rate > 20% → H6's rich arm is additionally built at D_min = 3 as a pre-committed sensitivity arm, and both results are reported side by side. Direction must survive both for any headline. > 35% → H6 scoring halts pending rubric revision.”

**The tripwire did NOT fire.** The measured part-2 FOLLOW-UP overturn rate is **0.183333** (18.33%) against the frozen > 20% line quoted above. The D_min = 3 sensitivity arm was pre-committed **conditionally**, and its condition was not met, so it is not built. Development's own rate was 25%, above the line, which is why the appendix expected the arm to fire; the confirmatory measurement came in below it.

To be explicit about what is and is not on the record: the root-excluded arm in section 4 is a **different** sensitivity arm (owner ruling 1, unconditional). The D_min = 3 arm is the one the tripwire governs, and it does not exist.

> **Frozen bar, quoted verbatim** (PREREGISTRATION_AMENDMENT_2.md B2, Pre-written readings (equal prominence)):

**Neither reading is applied.** Why: the branch rule returns DESCRIPTIVE at the primary budget (24 eligible subjects), and BOTH pre-written readings are hypothesis-level claims — the positive one asserts an effect, the null one asserts a publishable absence of one. A descriptive result supports neither. Both stay quoted above at equal prominence with no claim attached to either, and the measured directions and confidence intervals sit beside them as descriptive numbers.

The null reading in particular has to be EARNED, not defaulted to. It asserts a publishable absence of an effect, which takes a powered null. This run is not one: the primary model's registered contrast is +0.0217 cosine at p = 0.2169 over 24 subjects, and the direction is POSITIVE in all four model × channel cells at the primary budget. A non-significant positive point estimate on a small sample is an absence of evidence, not evidence of absence.

The dose check cuts against a null reading from the other side as well: the primary model's sign REVERSES at B = 400 (-0.0230 cosine, p = 0.0101). A contrast that changes direction with the budget is not a settled null either — even at a higher branch, neither reading could have been applied on this evidence without more of it.

**The operative fact of this H6 run is the eligibility shortfall itself.** Development supply implied roughly two thirds (4 of 6) of the pool would be eligible at B = 1,000; the confirmatory corpus delivered 24 of 88 (27%). H6 is therefore **UNRESOLVED at confirmatory scale on this corpus** — not answered positive, not answered null. What this run establishes is that the registered H6 design does not reach confirmatory power on MediaSum-derived grounding transcripts at the frozen budget, and that is the finding to carry forward.

#### An unplanned reproducibility check, and what it found

Those identical arms produce prompts that hash identically to the registered run's, so 72 of the same prompts were generated twice — same model, same weights, same temperature 0.0, same seed 0 — in two separate Leonardo jobs. That is a free run-to-run reproducibility check nobody planned, and it does not come out clean.

- Byte-identical generated answers: **15 of 72**.
- Channel-1 cosine differs on **57 of 72**: median absolute gap 0.013822, mean 0.024381, largest 0.123078.
- Channel-2 stance label differs on **4 of 72**.

**Why, and what it does and does not mean.** Greedy decoding is deterministic in arithmetic but not across batch compositions: vLLM's batched matrix multiplies reduce in an order that depends on what else is in the batch, and a single flipped token early in a 150-word free-text answer changes everything after it. The registered job batched 542 prompts; this job batched 182, so the batches were never the same. This is a property of the instrument, not a bug in either run, and it was present in every number in this report before this arm existed.

What it adds is a **magnitude for that noise on this corpus: about 0.013822 cosine at the median item**, run to run, on free-text answers of this length. Item noise averages down into a subject mean and again across subjects, so it does not translate one-for-one into a contrast, but it is the same order as several of the differences reported above. It is one more reason the registered contrasts read as descriptive rather than decisive, and it is recorded here because this arm is where it happened to become measurable — not as a criticism of the registered numbers, which were computed correctly from the run that produced them.

### What it shows

The rich arm leans on shallow chains for its supply. Raising the depth requirement from 2 to 3 cuts the eligible pool from 24 subjects to 12 at B = 1,000, and from 41 to 32 at B = 400 — and that is despite 76 of the 88 subjects carrying items having at least one depth ≥ 3 chain somewhere in their transcripts. Having a deep chain and having 1,000 words of them are very different things. Hosts in this corpus drill about two turns and move on, so most of the registered rich arm's volume is depth-2 chains plus their NEW-TOPIC roots.

Taking the poor arm out of it isolates the rich arm's own supply, and it roughly halves: of the 88 subjects carrying items, 45 can fill a rich arm at B = 1,000 with depth ≥ 2 and 23 can with depth ≥ 3; at B = 400 it is 52 against 43. The joint eligibility counts fall by about the same proportion, so the rich arm — not the poor one — is what the depth requirement bites.

On direction: at B = 1,000 the deeper arm is positive on channel 1 (+0.0459 cosine, n = 12) against a positive registered contrast, and at B = 400 it is negative (-0.0145 cosine, n = 32) against a negative registered contrast.

The surviving subjects are also the least informative ones. 3 of the 12 arms at B = 1,000 and 14 of the 32 at B = 400 are byte-identical to the registered rich arm — for those subjects raising D_min removed nothing, because everything that fitted the budget was already depth ≥ 3. So the exploratory arm is not a clean 'deeper material' arm: it is a smaller, partly overlapping subset of the registered one, and at B = 400 nearly half of it is the registered arm verbatim.

Read it as supply diagnostics, not as an effect. Every interval below crosses zero or sits on a dozen-odd subjects, the branch forbids a hypothesis-test claim at any depth, and this arm was not pre-committed — the tripwire that would have pre-committed it did not fire. What it adds to the record is the mechanism behind section 8's operative fact: H6's eligibility shortfall is not a quirk of one threshold, it gets worse as the depth requirement rises, because the depth-1 and depth-2 material the registered rich arm leans on is most of what this corpus has.
