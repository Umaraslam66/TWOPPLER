# Owner rulings — 2026-07-28

**Name-resolution defects: DEFERRED.** Two subjects failed the Stage 2
confirmatory build because their canonical name never resolved to a speaker in
their test transcript, so the build found zero guest turns. `C02240` ("St. John")
is the single-token canonical class: the surname carries a particle, the
canonical key collapses to one token (`john`), and the matcher cannot bind it to
a speaker line. `C02521` ("Wong Ulrich") is the compound-surname class: a
two-token surname that the corpus spells inconsistently across transcripts.
Both are recorded in the confirmatory report's name-resolution section
(`results/stage2_confirm/STAGE2_CONFIRM_REPORT.md`, section 6) and in the build
tripwires in `results/stage2_confirm/build_full140.json`
(`tripwires.zero_guest_turns_in_test` for both, `tripwires.subject_key_single_token`
for C02240, with the neighbouring `tripwires.subject_variant_token_collapse`
holding the wider compound-surname family).

**No mid-project pool amendment.** The candidate pool is not being edited, the
matcher is not being changed, and no subject is being re-admitted. Both subjects
failed before generation, so nothing downstream of the build has to move, and
the attrition is already visible in the survival rate rather than absorbed into
it. Changing name resolution now would alter the drawn cohort after the draw,
which is the thing the frozen draw procedure exists to prevent.

**Carried as one documented-deviation candidate for a future corpus version.**
The two defect classes are recorded together as a single candidate item for any
later rebuild of the corpus — not as a Stage 2 deviation, because nothing in
Stage 2 deviates: the build rule was applied as written and these two subjects
did not survive it. **No action is taken in this project cycle.**
